About This Game

Innovative targeting and shooting mechanics, a unique "Marksmanship" system, fully customizable firearms with operable components at every stage, and personalized magazines and ammunition. – the tactical shooter “Unlimited Defense” is coming soon.

In a world where firearms are a passion, you will become a young shooting enthusiast who has just obtained a firearms license, chasing the dream of becoming a professional shooter. Immerse yourself in the passion, discipline, and culture of competitive shooting.

A、IPSC：

Travel to authentic shooting ranges around the globe and experience competitive shooting disciplines inspired by real-world competitions.

With guidance from IPSC China and inspiration from international shooting sports, Unlimited Defense aims to recreate professional match structures, stage designs, and competitive formats with high attention to detail.

From IPSC-inspired matches to IDPA, USPSA, PCSL, 3Gun, CQB, Double Tap, and more, the game will continue expanding its competitive shooting experiences — including future online and offline tournaments.

 

 

 

Various firearms: Classic、Open、Production、Production Optic、PCC、Revolver、Standard... ...

Classic, Open, Production, Production Optic, PCC, Revolver, Standard... ...

 

 

 

Various targets: bullseye, popper, A4 target, moving, animal, Mickey Mouse target, Texas Star, flying disc, simulation

 

 Almost all optical characteristics of sights are reproduced: Open sight, Aperture sight,Ghost ring sight， Red dot, Holographic,Collimator, Prism, ACOG, Combination sights, Telescopic sights, etc.

B、Shooting Range Creator:

Highly Customizable Range and Competition Editor

（Under Development – For Reference Only）

The entire series of firearms :

Unlimited Defense is dedicated to recreating the atmosphere of real-world shooting environments, from range layouts and architectural structures to shooting area designs, all carefully crafted with reference to authentic shooting ranges. Players will compete in realistic competitive shooting environments and experience an immersive shooting experience inspired by professional shooting facilities.
